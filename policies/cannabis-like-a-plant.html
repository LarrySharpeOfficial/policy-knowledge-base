Regulate cannabis like an ordinary plant

Source-backed summary

Larry uses onions as the comparison for ordinary-plant regulation. He also calls for pardons and expungement for cannabis-only offenses and ordinary sales-tax treatment.

Verification boundary

This summary excludes improvised licensing examples and does not invent missing safety or implementation rules. Readers should check the linked recording against the auto-generated transcript.

Proposal

Treat cannabis like an ordinary plant and product, clear records for cannabis-only offenses, and apply ordinary sales taxation rather than a special cannabis tax structure.
